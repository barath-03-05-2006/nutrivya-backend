Privacy Policy for Nutrivya

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Nutrivya we respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number
  • Health and diet-related information you provide (meals, nutrition data, water intake, weight logs, progress photos)
  • Usage data (app interactions, for improving the service)

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your account and diet/nutrition tracking features
  • To connect you with your assigned dietitian, where applicable
  • To communicate with you about your account
  • To improve the app's functionality

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your data may be shared with your assigned dietitian as part of the app's core functionality.

Data Retention

We retain your account and health/diet information for as long as your account remains active on Nutrivya. Users cannot delete their own accounts directly within the app. To request deletion of your account or personal data, please contact us at divyanutrivya@gmail.com. Upon a verified request, your data will be reviewed and deleted by an administrator within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Children's Privacy

Nutrivya is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at divyanutrivya@gmail.com.
